# Protocol application note

The three files in `protocols/` preserve the domain-specific scoring
instructions and anchors used for the study. References to “Phase 1” and
“Phase 2” describe separation of the `OA` instrument from the `E`/`D`/`F`
instrument; they do not mean that the same people completed both phases in the
reported study.

The implemented design used non-overlapping pools:

1. Group A received prompts and responses and provided `OA` scores without the
   `E`/`D`/`F` rubric.
2. Group B received the diagnostic-dimension rubric and provided `E`, `D`, and
   `F` scores without providing `OA`.
3. Group B jointly reviewed the construct definitions and anchor examples
   before rating, then completed judgments independently.
4. Model identity was hidden during annotation.

The mental-health protocol includes a resource-constrained same-rater
alternative for future studies. That alternative was not used for the corpus
reported in the paper.

The rubrics use the annotation label “Contextual Fit.” The manuscript reports
the unchanged construct as “Domain Fit” to distinguish bounded study domains
from broader theories of context. No score or anchor was changed by this
terminology update.
