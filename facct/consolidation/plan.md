# Computational-only consolidation specification

Date: 2 October 2026. Written after inspection of all original and amendment results.
This is reporting and verification on explored evidence, not preregistration.

## Scope freeze

No new ratings, participants, model calls, thresholds, classifier families, equivalence
margins, or primary comparisons. Preserve the original three-domain H-minus-random
D>=3 family and its adjusted intervals. Trained and common-queue findings remain
exploratory. Keep full-domain and held-out capacities separate.

## Required checks

1. Cross-tabulate recorded export/rated-text equality against the stored join tier.
   Check the equality flag against the original source strings and hashes. Equality
   authenticates available strings, not the historical scorer invocation.
2. Describe full, retained byte-identical, excluded, and embedded-only batches by
   domain, condition, model, and scenario, including D/F/OA concern and prompt counts.
3. Independently reconstruct the existing same-capacity and same-fraction subset
   operating points, using the explicit byte-equality mask rather than join tier.
   Do not add selectively chosen tests or interpret composition changes causally.
4. Generate a compact provenance table and headline claim ledger from frozen outputs.
5. Rerun all four existing analysis stages in a separate snapshot/fresh environment;
   compare every numerical CSV and deterministic figure with the frozen originals.
   Keep runtime reports/manifests separate from numerical equality checks.
6. Generate a local-only computational candidate, excluding source text and personal
   records until release authority is confirmed. State its reproduction limits.

## Manuscript decision

Lead with comparative allocation, bounded incremental benefit, and coverage within
a shared queue. The card is supporting documentation, not an evaluated contribution.
Retain the transparent trained comparator; do not claim comparison against semantic
judges, modern evaluator systems generally, or the best available screen.

## Gates that computation cannot close

Historical scorer-input authentication, exact portal upload, institutional scope,
redistribution authority, confidential venue guidance, novelty judgment, and full
multi-file PDF compilation are distinct from successful statistical tests.
