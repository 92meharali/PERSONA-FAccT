# Criterion-to-decision amendment

Specified 28 October 2026 before executing this module. This is an exploratory
amendment on previously inspected data, not a preregistration or independent
confirmation. Existing predictions and within-criterion results are known.

## Question and fixed design

Does changing the concern label used to train a screen change its queue and its
coverage of other concerns? This connects the existing PERSONA measurement
diagnostic to review allocation, without asserting construct validation or a new
algorithm. The original three HumT-versus-random primary comparisons are retained.

- Train separately on original mean D >= 3, F < 4, and OA < 4. Use the existing
  five prompt-group folds, C=1 logistic model, training-only standardization and
  no tuning. Primary exploratory model: markers plus log1p(word length).
  Retain the same model plus imported H as a feature-set sensitivity, not a
  substitute chosen after seeing results.
- Evaluate every training-target by evaluation-target cell in each domain,
  including all diagonal and off-diagonal cells. Add the resolved mental-health
  contextual-reviewworthiness labels ONLY as evaluation outcomes. They cannot
  train, tune, choose, calibrate or weight a screen. No new participant evidence.
- Budgets 5%, 10%, 20%, 30%; existing 20% reference, not optimized. Each held-out
  fold is its own batch. Floor its quota and use the existing stable-ID ties.
  Never pool scores from separately fitted models into one domain ranking.
- Retain high-H, length, markers and analytical uniform-random comparators in
  these same batches. Quotas therefore differ from the full-domain simulation.
- Report counts, recall, precision, missed cases, unselected prevalence and
  matched-random differences. Report queue intersections, symmetric differences,
  Jaccard similarity and changes in captured concerns for every target pair.
- Compare each pair of training targets against each evaluation criterion;
  report all pairs, not a winner selected on the follow-up. Main interpretive
  bridge is F versus OA because their prior predictive overlap is known.
  Different cutoffs/prevalence are part of these operational target changes;
  they do not isolate a pure construct effect or prove normative disagreement.

## Uncertainty and checks

Use 2,000 paired within-fold prompt-cluster resamples, with the existing seed and
whole prompt copies kept in their original folds. Refit every model/scaler and
rerank at the replicate's exact per-fold capacity. Intervals are exploratory
pointwise 95% percentiles conditional on the partition, labels, model and corpus;
they do not cover rater uncertainty or form a new confirmatory comparison family.
Report zero-positive resamples and finite counts, and single-class training
fallbacks. Reuse the ten existing alternative partitions as point sensitivity.
No best-seed selection. Other capacities have point estimates only.

Test off-diagonal scoring, exact capacity, label separation, prompt integrity,
undefined endpoints, score ties, paired resampling, and agreement of reconstructed
diagonal predictions/counts with the prior conditional analysis. Preserve every
source and previous output hash. Save all new outputs separately.

## Interpretation gate

Integrate a connecting results section only after inspecting the full matrix.
Queue changes alone establish allocation sensitivity, not better review. A
directional external-target claim requires its paired interval and positive
support to be reported; 19 follow-up cases cannot validate a broad framework.
If transfer is weak or uncertain, say so and keep the contribution a bounded
measurement-use audit. Do not optimize targets, features, mixtures or language
for acceptance. Do not hide author involvement in the original ratings.

No API calls, new human/AI annotations, source corrections or changed ratings are
authorized by this computational amendment. A semantic comparator remains absent.
