## Genuine additions in the completed computation

| Added object | What it establishes that CHI's reported results do not directly establish | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Exact-budget allocation policy | Which labeled cases enter a constrained review queue and which remain unreviewed | Offline opportunity for detection, not observed review success |
| Matched random and observable baselines | Whether HumT adds allocation value beyond random, length and lexical markers | Marker cues overlap with D's rubric; not independent harm measurement |
| Separate D/F/OA operating points | Criterion-specific recall/precision and residual unreviewed prevalence | Thresholds are analytic choices, not validated normative standards |
| Workload to target recall | Retrospective proportion of a batch needed to recover a stated share of cases | A derived view of the ranking, not an independent experiment |
| Paired uncertainty and sensitivity | Uncertainty in policy differences, with threshold/consensus/tie/join variation | Same corpus and judgments; computation does not create new observations |
| Audit-use card | An explicit proposal to expose criteria, resource assumptions and challenge routes | Unevaluated design proposal; no usability or governance-effectiveness evidence |

Low linear predictive R2 does not mathematically determine top-budget recall for thresholded criteria. Thus these new operating points cannot simply be read off CHI's headline tables. However, this technical distinction alone does not establish a separately publishable scholarly contribution.

## What is fully shared or closely related

All 1,490 responses, 499 prompt clusters, recorded H values and human judgments are reused. The same domain/rater/source limitations apply. The conceptual concern that tone is not equivalent to contextual appropriateness is shared. F/OA screening results, some domain interpretations and the governance motivation are especially close to the CHI argument. No new participants, independent raters, model outputs or agent experiments are included.

## Recommended contribution boundary

Lead with **criterion-specific resource allocation and comparison against feasible cheap policies**, including cases where HumT helps and cases where alternatives help more. Treat F/OA results as supporting comparisons, not a rewritten headline that H alone is insufficient. Define non-selection as unreviewed, never safe. Present the reporting card as a proposal only. Disclose the complete resource reuse prominently and avoid claiming a new annotation framework or dataset.

The computational draft's distinct question is: "Given a fixed review capacity and an explicitly chosen concern criterion, what allocation opportunities and misses result from competing observable policies?" Its contribution is a bounded policy comparison and transparent decision accounting, not proof that the metric fails, a human validation study, or a complete assurance audit.

## Verdict and decision paths

**The analyses are genuinely new, but independent-publication distinctness remains uncertain and overlap risk is substantial.** This is a computational extension candidate. It is not defensible to mark the concurrency issue resolved merely by changing metrics or rearranging prose.

1. Continue internal refinement using the precise contribution boundary above; this does not require claiming eligibility.
2. The author will handle chair contact. Provide the submitted CHI PDF and an honest computational-only disclosure through the confidential mechanism the chairs specify; do not rely on a public identifying repository link.
3. If the chairs provide applicable guidance supporting consideration of distinct related work, comply with the required disclosures and reassess the final manuscript against that scope. Such guidance is not an acceptance promise or unlimited permission for later scope changes.
4. If guidance is negative, unclear or absent while CHI remains under review, the conservative course is to defer a potentially overlapping full submission, not interpret silence as approval. Do not withdraw CHI automatically.
5. Even after CHI review ends, novelty and prior-publication overlap still need assessment if CHI is accepted; the disappearance of concurrent review does not erase shared contributions.

The local assessment is complete. The external eligibility decision is not, and cannot be completed by the assistant alone.
