# External-study launch checklist

All items start pending unless explicitly confirmed below. A draft file is not evidence of approval.

| Item | Status | Required evidence or decision |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment scope | Author confirmed | 2-3 independent practitioners in each of three domains |
| Ethics coverage for this new activity | Pending | Written institutional confirmation or amendment; store identifying record privately |
| Responsible study lead and interviewer | Pending | Name/contact recorded privately, conflict and supervisory-role check |
| Two human coders | Pending | Identify coders, agree independent coding subset and preserve disagreements |
| Compensation | Pending | Amount/currency/method, partial-session terms and funding; do not assume original-study terms |
| Consent and optional recording | Pending | Approved consent; notes-only alternative; quotation permission separate |
| Withdrawal and retention | Pending | Concrete cutoff/process, retention duration, deletion/access rules |
| Data storage | Pending | Institution-approved restricted location outside public Git; separate identities and notes |
| Sensitive-content support | Pending | Reviewed warning, skip/stop procedure, researcher and independent ethics contacts |
| Collection window | Pending | Fixed recruitment end date and availability-based 6-9 target before outcomes are inspected |
| Packet review | Pending human review | Read all selected text; check content and possible model-identity disclosure without silently altering the sample |
| Internal timing rehearsal | Pending | Record duration and amendments; researchers are not external participants |
| Final launch authorization | Pending | Author approval after preceding gates; current permission covers preparation only |
| CHI/FAccT related-submission guidance | Pending, separate gate | Unsent drafts exist; do not contact or submit without authorization |

## Questions to take to the institution

Does the approved parent project's scope cover new practitioner interviews about AI-review criteria and reporting artifacts, including potentially distressing case text? If not, what amendment or new review is required? Does proposed audio recording, de-identified quotation, compensation and data retention fit the approved scope?

Supply the actual protocol, consent draft and case materials. Do not describe them as already approved or rely on the parent project's title alone. No institutional contact has been made by the agent.
