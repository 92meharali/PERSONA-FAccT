# External-evaluator study protocol (NOT CONDUCTED)

Version 1.0, 2 October 2026. These are preparation materials, not an approved protocol or collected evidence. Do not recruit until the institution confirms coverage or issues the required amendment and the authors authorize recruitment. No external participant judgments exist in this package.

Operational addendum: [session plan v1.1](session_plan.md) records the author's confirmed 2-3 practitioners per domain, exact session timing and packet rules. It clarifies the general v1.0 plan below; neither version is a preregistration or ethics approval. Start with the [launch checklist](launch_checklist.md) and [prepared packets](materials/README.md).

## Purpose and sample

Investigate whether response-level review allocation is a plausible activity for evaluators, which criteria warrant review, and whether an audit-use card communicates what selection and non-selection mean. Initially seek 6-9 external practitioners/evaluators, approximately 2-3 with relevant experience in each domain. This is a bounded purposive qualitative sample justified by the decision questions and available expertise, not representative, statistically powered or presumed saturated. If only one domain can be recruited, narrow the study explicitly and amend before seeing outcomes.

Eligible participants have relevant evaluation, governance or domain-review experience that they can describe; do not infer licensure from job titles. Exclude original authors, original rubric developers, original raters and research interns involved in PERSONA from any sample described as external. Screen conflicts and prior exposure. Practitioners do not represent affected users, and this design cannot establish user harm or acceptable tradeoffs for all populations.

## Two stages, different evidentiary roles

1. Formative criterion review (about 20 minutes): interview about real tasks, budget constraints and escalation; show construct definitions without thresholds or study performance. Ask what deserves review and why. Use a small neutral randomly selected orientation sample, not the highest-H or worst-outcome examples. Log proposed changes and reasons. Lock this record before showing performance. Amendments remain distinguishable from v1 criteria; never rewrite original criteria as preregistered.
2. Artifact interpretation (about 25-35 minutes): show a counterbalanced set of blind cases and audit-use cards. Evaluate understanding of recall versus precision, non-selection versus clearance, and uncertainty. Give equal time to benefits and misses. Ask participants to explain a disputed decision, not merely endorse the framework. This stage following formative exposure is an informed usability critique, not an independent confirmatory validation sample. Independent follow-up evaluation would need a separately recruited group and a frozen artifact/protocol.

Use 6-8 cases per participant, matched to their expertise where possible, chosen by the predeclared sampling register rather than investigator preference. The computational case sample deliberately covers selected concerns, unreviewed concerns and selected cases outside the criterion for each policy/domain/criterion; it is not prevalence-representative. Empty cells stay empty. Duplicate cases across strata are shown once. Preserve text exactly and hide model labels, scores and sampling category on the first pass. Inspect for model identity revealed in the text; log inability to blind rather than silently edit it. No crisis-response guidance is being delivered to participants.

Rotate policy/card order A-B-C, B-C-A, C-A-B by enrollment number; retain assigned order. Present counts and matched-budget comparators before interpretive claims. Do not label a policy "best". Permit refusal of individual cases and stopping without penalty. Sensitive-content warning and support/contact procedure must be institutionally reviewed.

## Data and analysis

With separate consent, record audio or use written notes. Store identifying consent records, contact lists and raw recordings outside the repository in an institution-approved restricted location. Pseudonymize excerpts; review re-identification and source-text redistribution risks. Retention, access, withdrawal cutoff and compensation must be completed in the consent template before recruitment.

Two human coders independently code a documented subset: all pilot transcripts and at least 25% of remaining material, spread across domains/participant roles, with at least two transcripts when available. Log the denominator, double-coded units, initial disagreements and codebook revisions. Retain initial codes before discussion. Descriptive agreement can accompany interpretive analysis; it is not evidence of objective normative truth. Report discrepant and negative cases. AI-generated candidate codes are aids only, never independent coding or participant evidence.

Deliverables after actual collection: recruitment flow, experience descriptions without invented credentials, protocol deviations, criterion-dispute table, artifact comprehension errors, differing cost judgments, and limitations of missing affected-user perspectives. No saturation claim without an explicit defensible assessment.

## Stop conditions

No approval/scope confirmation: no recruitment. No consent: no collection. Insufficient time: report materials pending, not "validation underway" as a result. If practitioners reject the proposed workflow, narrow or abandon operational claims rather than recruit until endorsement appears.
