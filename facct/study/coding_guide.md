# Candidate coding guide (not applied by human coders)

AI-assisted preparatory framework. No completed human codes or coding agreement are claimed. Unit: a case-level explanation or a coherent interview segment; retain transcript/case ID and location. Multiple codes may apply; use explicit uncertainty rather than force classification.

| Candidate code | Inclusion rule | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow plausibility | Firsthand task/decision resembles proposed review allocation | Separate hypothetical endorsement from actual practice |
| Review criterion | Reason a response should/should not enter review | Not necessarily an experienced harm |
| Attribution | AI self-presentation versus quoted/third-party language | Do not score user quotations as AI commitments |
| Internal-state cue | Claimed feeling, understanding, intention or memory | Taxonomy-informed description, not automatic deception |
| Social positioning | Claimed role, relationship or authority | Distinguish functional assistance from human identity |
| Contextual mismatch | Action, tone, certainty or boundary conflicts with the situation | Keep rationale independent of a low F label |
| Non-selection interpretation | What a participant infers from absence of a flag | "False reassurance" requires explicit clearance interpretation |
| Cost allocation | Time, missed-case consequences, unnecessary-review burden | Identify whose cost and whether measured or assumed |
| Criterion dispute | Objection to definition, anchor or aggregation | Preserve disagreement rather than code as misunderstanding |
| Artifact comprehension | Interpretation of counts, budgets, uncertainty and comparisons | Distinguish conceptual dissent from arithmetic error |
| Contestation feasibility | Who could challenge/change a decision and how | Proposal is not demonstrated effectiveness |
| Missing perspective | Affected user, language, culture or stakeholder absent | Practitioner does not automatically represent that group |
| Negative case | Evidence against expected benefit or chosen design | Must be retained in the report |

The internal-state/social-positioning vocabulary is informed by DeVrio et al.'s taxonomy, not a validated mapping to D/F/OA; see the verified literature memo. Other taxonomy lenses may be added with an amendment, not forced onto every case.

## Independent coding and reconciliation

Two human coders receive the same de-identified subset without each other's codes. Preserve initial codes, timestamps and uncertainty. Compare disagreements at the specified unit, then discuss interpretations and revise the codebook with a version log. Report raw agreement/counts only with the denominator and code prevalence; if reporting a reliability coefficient, justify its assumptions. Consensus is a subsequent analytic step, not retroactive independent agreement. AI must not be one of the two coders.

The generated human_coding_template.csv is blank. cases_blinded.csv omits scores/model labels; cases_answer_key.csv is for the coordinator and later analysis. Keep participant-derived files out of public outputs until approved de-identification and disclosure review.
