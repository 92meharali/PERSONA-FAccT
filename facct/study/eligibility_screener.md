# Eligibility screener (draft, not sent)

Use only after approval and recruitment authorization. Store completed forms and contacts in the approved private system, not this repository. Request only the information needed to assess experience, independence and logistics; do not collect unnecessary license numbers or sensitive personal history.

1. Which area best matches your work: mental health, general health or education? Briefly describe your actual role and responsibilities.
2. Have you reviewed, supervised or evaluated advice/content in that area? Give a short example without identifying a patient, client, learner or employer-confidential material.
3. Have you evaluated AI-generated responses before? If yes, describe your actual task. If no, that does not automatically exclude you.
4. Were you an author, annotator, rubric developer or involved research intern for the original PERSONA project? If yes, you are not eligible for this external sample.
5. Have you already seen PERSONA results/materials? Have you collaborated with, been supervised by, or held a dependent working relationship with the recruiting researcher? Record the details privately for review rather than silently counting the person as fully independent.
6. Are you comfortable considering examples that may include distress, health advice or educational boundary problems, with the option to skip any example or stop? A refusal has no penalty; no explanation or personal history is required.
7. Are you available for approximately 60 minutes within [approved collection window]? Preferred access arrangements: [optional].
